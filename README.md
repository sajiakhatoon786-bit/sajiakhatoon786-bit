### 📌 Featured Spatial & Data Projects

#### 🗺️ [1. Demographic Classification & Spatial Literacy Disparity (QGIS)](https://github.com/sajiakhatoon786-bit/West-Bengal-literacy-cartography)
- **Dataset:** Census Demographic Tables (19 Administrative Districts of West Bengal)
- **Focus:** Data Normalization, Spatial Cartography & Statistical Break Selection
- Identified and resolved the *"raw count trap"* by normalizing total literate counts against district populations into standardized literacy rates.
- Replaced arbitrary equal-interval splits with **Jenks Natural Breaks** optimization, accurately surfacing regional performance tiers and south-central educational access corridors.
- Delivered an executive print layout featuring locator map insets, custom text halos for contrast, and dynamic legend formatting.

#### 📊 [2. Metric Proximity & Spatial Catchment Modeling (QGIS)](https://github.com/sajiakhatoon786-bit?tab=repositories)
- **Focus:** CRS Coordinate Transformation & Buffer Operations
- Structured spatial workflows by converting datasets from geographic coordinates (WGS84 degrees) into localized projected coordinate reference systems (UTM metric grids).
- Built linear buffer models and spatial joins to determine catchment areas and proximity zones for infrastructure planning.
